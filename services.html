Before we start

Good questions.

What does a website cost?+

The quote depends on the pages, content, and features. Tell me about the project so we can define a useful scope before discussing a price.

Can we work in Spanish?+

Yes. We can collaborate in English or Spanish, and discuss a bilingual website when it fits your audience.

What should I have ready?+

Your business name, a short description, your services, and any existing logo or photos. We can identify the remaining content together.

Can you update an existing site?+

Yes. Start by sharing the current link and what you want to improve. I’ll review the structure before proposing changes.
