# Brand guide

## Identity

**Name:** Norvak Web  
**Voice:** Direct, personal, creative, and easy to understand.  
**Tagline:** Modern, functional websites that load fast and feel good.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Charcoal `#101112` | Main dark surfaces |
| Text | Off-white `#F5F5F0` | Primary text |
| Primary accent | Neon green `#2FFF6B` | Primary actions and selected states |
| Secondary accent | Neon pink `#FF2F92` | Small highlights and brand details |

Use charcoal and white for most of the page. Neon accents should support hierarchy, readability, and personality. Avoid competing accent colors on every element. Choose text colors according to the background and verify contrast during implementation.

## Logo

Use the approved angular Norvak N consistently across the site and repository branding. The updated direction uses pink and green. A dark smoky treatment can serve as a large brand image; small placements need a rendition that remains legible at their actual display size.

Retain the N’s geometry and proportions. Keep sufficient clear space, avoid stretching, and avoid dropping a bright rectangular background into a dark header. Add the final approved source files in the implementation phase, with names that describe their role.

## Typography and imagery

Use a clear sans-serif family with a consistent heading hierarchy and comfortable body copy. A geometric family such as the Manrope used in the reference version is a starting point, subject to review in the new layout. Include font licenses when self-hosting.

Use real project imagery or verified screenshots. Label previews and illustrative project imagery accurately. Keep backgrounds minimal, with enough space for the work and copy to stand out.
