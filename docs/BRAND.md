# Brand guide

## Identity

**Name:** Norvak Web  
**Voice:** Direct, personal, creative, and easy to understand.  
**Tagline:** Beautiful. Simple. Effective.

## Palette

| Role | Color | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Charcoal `#101112` | Main dark surfaces |
| Text | Off-white `#F5F5F0` | Primary text |
| Primary accent | Neon green `#2FFF6B` | Primary actions and selected states |
| Secondary accent | Neon pink `#FF2F92` | Small highlights and brand details |

Use charcoal and white for most of the page. Neon accents should support hierarchy, readability, and personality. Avoid competing accent colors on every element. Choose text colors according to the background and verify contrast during implementation.

## Logo

Use the approved angular Norvak N consistently across the site and repository branding. The updated direction uses pink and green. A dark smoky treatment can serve as a large brand image; small placements need a rendition that remains legible at their actual display size.

Retain the N’s geometry and proportions. Keep sufficient clear space, avoid stretching, and avoid dropping a bright rectangular background into a dark header.

The imported baseline uses `assets/norvak-n-clean.webp` for small header/footer placements and its transparent PNG for the favicon. `assets/norvak-n-textured.webp` is used in large Home/About placements. These are the existing site’s renditions; review the unified logo treatment as part of the next design pass.

## Typography and imagery

Use a clear sans-serif family with a consistent heading hierarchy and comfortable body copy. A geometric family such as the Manrope used in the reference version is a starting point, subject to review in the new layout. Include font licenses when self-hosting.

Use real project imagery or verified screenshots. Label previews and illustrative project imagery accurately. Keep backgrounds minimal, with enough space for the work and copy to stand out.

## Brand promise

Premium websites built for a world that moves fast. Norvak creates sites that look polished, feel effortless, and get people straight to what matters. Clear navigation, easy access, fast loading, and a clear next action guide every design decision.

Respect the visitor’s time: concise copy, purposeful sections, readable typography, generous spacing, and restrained motion. Every element should help someone understand the business or take the next step. Avoid clutter and unsupported claims about results or delivery speed.

**Spanish tagline:** Diseño atractivo. Simple. Eficaz.

**Pitch:** People have no time to waste. Norvak builds premium websites that get straight to the point: beautiful design, effortless access, and a clear path to action.
